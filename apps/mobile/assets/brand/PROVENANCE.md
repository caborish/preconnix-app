# PreConnix brand assets

Approved Flourish brand mark (owner, 1 Nov 2026), from the PreConnix Flourish icon kit (30 Oct 2026). It replaces the
earlier six-petal trace.

- `flourish.svg`: the four-petal symbol in colour (upper-right petal rose, the others violet). Used next to the wordmark.
- `flourish-reversed.svg`: white petals with the rose accent, for dark surfaces.
- `preconnix-app-icon-1024.png`: the kit's launcher PNG, unchanged (white and rose Flourish on deep violet, square corners;
  the platform applies its mask).
- `preconnix-adaptive-foreground-1024.png`: the kit's Android adaptive foreground SVG rendered to a transparent 1024 PNG.
  Paired with the deep-violet background #5C3A91.

The kit notes the brand vector is a reconstruction of the approved concept with exact flat colours, not an untouched
original master. Keep its proportions, orientation and central gap.
